# Product direction

Patent2Market helps a nontechnical founder turn a problem into a credible, testable business plan. Journal publication is not a product objective.

The product should reduce the decisions the user has to make while preserving their control of consequential choices. AI is a thinking partner: clarify the customer, translate technical mechanisms, combine technology with delivery models, identify disconfirming evidence, challenge economics, and create an editable plan grounded in the user's evidence.

Sustainability and responsible practice belong in ordinary design and operations: repairability, resource use, affordability, fair terms, and substantiated claims.

Current high-priority gaps:

1. Configure a hosted inference backend; local Qwen inference has been verified, but the hosted site still needs its own model connection.
2. Add grounded live research and source collection with citations and provenance.
3. Establish a verified patent-access pipeline: identifiers, family members, territory, expiration/legal events, open license conditions, and date of verification. Never present the NASA snapshot as a free-to-use patent catalog.
4. Improve need-to-technology matching with evaluated semantic retrieval and AI-assisted candidate comparison, including the option that no patent is needed.
5. Test with actual founders, including whether a smaller, cheaper solution addresses the need better than the suggested patented approach.
6. Add authenticated public access, distributed provider quotas, and cost controls before a public AI-backed launch.
